# Mayer Haater Swaad | মায়ের হাতের স্বাদ 🌿

> **100% Organic · 100% Natural · 0% Preservatives**
> Farm Fresh Masalas Delivered Straight To Your Doorstep.

Welcome to the official repository for **Mayer Haater Swaad**, a premium front-end e-commerce web application. This platform is designed to seamlessly showcase and sell authentic, legacy stone-ground traditional Bengali spices directly to customers using a streamlined WhatsApp ordering system.

## ✨ Key Features

* **Instant WhatsApp Checkout:** Customers can add items to their cart, fill out their delivery details, and send a perfectly formatted order summary directly to the business WhatsApp number.
* **Dynamic Shopping Cart:** Real-time price calculation, weight variant selection, and quantity adjustments without page reloads.
* **Ultra-Modern UI/UX:** Built with a clean, responsive "glassmorphism" design, smooth scrolling, and interactive hover states.
* **Fully Responsive:** Works flawlessly on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.
* **Zero Backend Required:** Runs entirely on the client-side (browser), making it incredibly fast and free to host.

## 🌶️ Current Product Catalog

Our current catalog features hand-picked, organically grown ingredients sourced directly from Nadia and Murshidabad districts:

1. **Holud Guro (Turmeric):** Vibrant golden turmeric powder rich in natural curcumin.
2. **Jeera Guro (Cumin):** Hand-roasted cumin seeds ground to a warm, nutty profile.
3. **Dhania Guro (Coriander):** Freshly stone-ground with citrusy, earthy aromas.
4. **Lonka Guro (Red Chilli):** Coarsely ground sun-dried red chilli for intense heat.
